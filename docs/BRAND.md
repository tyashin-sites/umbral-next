# UMBRAL — brand book

> Demonstration house built by Tyashin × Thridify (25 Nov 2026) to show door & window
> manufacturers what their site could be. Umbral is invented. No real people, quotes,
> awards, addresses or phone numbers are claimed anywhere. Specifications are
> illustrative and the site says so in the footer and on /about.

## Name
**Umbral** — Spanish for *threshold*. It sits one letter from *umbra*, the darkest part of a
shadow. The house makes thresholds, and it thinks in light and shadow. Written UMBRAL
in the wordmark, Umbral in running text.

## Philosophy (internal, one line)
A threshold is the only part of a house you pass through. We make it the part you remember.

## Mission (public)
To make the line between inside and outside as thin, as quiet and as exact as it can be —
and then to make it open beautifully.

## What we believe (four beliefs, used on /about, never as a card grid)
1. **The frame is a shadow.** Every millimetre of frame is a millimetre of shadow on the floor
   at four in the afternoon. We count them.
2. **The opening is the product.** A door is judged in the two seconds it moves. Weight,
   travel, the sound it does not make.
3. **Weather is a guest.** Glass should let the season in and keep the weather out. Numbers
   prove that, adjectives don't.
4. **Show everything.** Every system can be configured, turned and opened on this site before
   anyone talks to us. A drawing is a promise; a model you can open is proof.

## Audience
Architects and their private clients building or restoring a serious house. They read
section drawings. They have seen every brochure. They decide in silence and call later.

## Voice
- **Exact.** Numbers over adjectives. "19 mm" beats "ultra-slim".
- **Quiet.** Short sentences. No exclamation marks. Never sells; describes.
- **Physical.** Talks about light, weight, sound, temperature, hands, floors, afternoons.
- **Dry warmth.** Allowed one turn of phrase per section, never two.
- Sentence case everywhere. Headlines ≤ 9 words. Body paragraphs ≤ 3 sentences.

**Do:** "The pane is 6 metres wide. The frame you see is 19 mm."
**Don't:** "Elevate your living space with seamless, timeless craftsmanship."

**Banned words:** elevate, unlock, seamless, timeless, empower, transform, reimagine,
curated, bespoke (we say *made to size*), luxury/luxurious (we never say it), stunning,
world-class, cutting-edge, game-changer, "experience the difference", journey.

## Visual identity
- **Palette** (dominant + one accent): Cal (limestone) `#ECE7DE` dominant · Tinta (ink)
  `#16140F` text and night sections · Umbra `#2A251E` deep surfaces · Bronce `#8A6A47` single
  accent (rules, links, focus) · Luz (daylight) `#F7F4EE` raised surfaces · Vidrio `#C7D0CE` used
  only for glass in drawings. FORBIDDEN: purple/indigo/cyan/neon, gradient text, glassmorphism,
  grey shadows.
- **Type**: Newsreader (display, weights 300/400 + italic; light at size, tight tracking) ·
  Hanken Grotesk (body and UI, 400/500) · IBM Plex Mono (every number: mm, m², dB, W/m²K).
  Why: an editorial serif carries the quiet; the grotesk keeps the UI architectural; the mono
  makes specification a visual material instead of a table nobody reads.
- **Lines, not boxes**: hairline rules in bronze at 40%; section drawings as the icon language
  (no Lucide-in-tinted-squares, no emoji). Radius 2 px almost everywhere.
- **Light**: shadows tinted warm (ink at low alpha, never grey). Grain on night sections only.

## Signature moments (three, no more)
1. **The shadow clock** (home hero): the shadow of a window's mullions falls across a
   limestone wall and moves with the visitor's local time of day; scrolling advances the
   afternoon. Pure CSS/SVG, the hero text is never hidden.
2. **The sightline** (home + every product): drag a section drawing from a conventional
   86 mm frame to the system's real sightline; the view through the drawn opening widens
   and the numbers run in mono.
3. **Open it** (every product page): the Thridify 3D model, poster-first, configurable and
   openable in the page. Hotspots trigger the real opening motion.

## Conversion
No cart. Luxury openings are made to size. Two paths on every product:
- **Configure** (in 3D, on the page) → **Request a quotation for this configuration**
  (contact form with product + chosen configuration pre-filled).
- **For architects**: section drawings and specification on request (form, typed "architect").
"Indicative from" prices are not shown — the house quotes per opening.
